# Simple Interest Calculator

A simple interest calculator that calculates the simple interest based on the principal amount, annual rate of interest, and time period in years.

## Input

- P – Principal amount
- R – Annual rate of interest
- T – Time period in years

## Formula

Simple Interest = (P × R × T) / 100

## Output

The calculator displays the simple interest based on the given inputs.
